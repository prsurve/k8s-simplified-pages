Your Kubernetes cluster runs containers just fine. But can it actually run an AI model?

Your cluster passed every container test. Can it run an AI model? Most can't, and nothing warns you. Kubernetes can schedule containers anywhere, but a GPU only works if each node advertises it. Without that, your AI pod sits in Pending with no clear error. This is one of the most common surprises when teams move AI workloads onto Kubernetes. Takeaway: before you deploy a model, run kubectl describe node and check the Allocatable section for a GPU count. Zero means your cluster is not ready yet.
