STORY

Here are the 5 things I check now when pods evict randomly: (1) kubectl describe node — look for DiskPressure, MemoryPressure, PIDPressure conditions at the bottom. (2) Ephemeral storage usage — run df -h on the node, check /var/lib/kubelet. (3) Check for pods without ephemeral-storage limits in their spec — especially logging sidecars or temp file writers. (4) Look at node allocatable resources vs actual usage — sometimes the kubelet reserves more than you expect. (5) Check for OOMKilled containers in kubectl describe pod — they cause evictions but the pod status might just say 'Evicted'. Set up Prometheus alerts for node conditions before you need them. The alert I use: kube_node_status_condition{condition='DiskPressure',status='true'} == 1. Saved me dozens of 2 AM pages.
