I was manually scaling pods every morning watching SQS queues — until I learned KEDA exists. KEDA is an event-driven autoscaler that watches real metrics like SQS queue depth and scales your pods automatically. No more guessing, no more manual kubectl scale commands at 6 AM. One ScaledObject YAML + AWS creds = autoscaling that actually works for background jobs. Save this for your next SQS-backed service. Drop 'YAML' below and I'll send you a full ScaledObject template for SQS. — @k8s.simplified · Kubernetes for DevOps beginners "I was manually scaling pods every morning watching SQS queues — until I learned KEDA exists. KEDA is an event-driven autoscaler. It scales your pods based on real metrics — like your SQS queue depth. First, install KEDA with Helm. Two commands and you're ready. Create a ScaledObject that watches your SQS queue. Set the queue name, AWS region, and how many messages per pod. KEDA uses IAM roles or secrets for AWS creds. Point it at your deployment, and it takes over from the default HPA. Now when your queue fills up, pods scale automatically. When it drains, they scale back down. Zero manual work. I wish I knew this two years ago. It would've saved me from so many 6 AM Slack pings."

YAML

Here's a full KEDA ScaledObject template for scaling based on SQS queue depth: apiVersion: keda.sh/v1alpha1 kind: ScaledObject metadata: name: sqs-scaledobject namespace: default spec: scaleTargetRef: name: your-deployment-name minReplicaCount: 1 maxReplicaCount: 10 triggers: - type: aws-sqs-queue metadata: queueURL: https://sqs.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/123456789012/your-queue queueLength: '5' awsRegion: us-east-1 identityOwner: pod Make sure your pod has an IAM role with sqs:GetQueueAttributes and sqs:ReceiveMessage permissions. If you're not using IRSA, add a TriggerAuthentication resource with AWS access keys instead. The queueLength of 5 means KEDA will try to keep roughly 5 messages per pod — tune this based on your processing time. Deploy this after installing KEDA and it'll take over scaling immediately.
