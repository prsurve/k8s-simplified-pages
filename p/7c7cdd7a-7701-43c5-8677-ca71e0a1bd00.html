PROBES

Here's the full probe setup that saved me from restart loops. StartupProbe gives your app 5 minutes to boot (30 failures × 10 seconds). Once it passes, livenessProbe takes over with a 15-second tolerance (3 failures × 5 seconds) to catch zombies without being trigger-happy. ReadinessProbe runs every 5 seconds with only 2 allowed failures so traffic only hits truly ready pods. Key insight: startupProbe's failureThreshold should cover your worst-case boot time plus buffer. For Java apps, I usually do 60 failures at 10s (10 min). For Go microservices, 10 failures at 3s (30 sec) is plenty. The mistake I made was skipping startupProbe entirely and setting liveness failureThreshold to 3, which gave my app only 15 seconds to start before K8s killed it. Use all three probes, tune them to your app's actual startup time, and test under load because cold starts behave differently than warm restarts.
