ROADMAP

Here's the order I'd actually learn Kubernetes in, no fluff: 1) Pods — the smallest deployable unit, understand what's actually running inside one (kubectl get pods, kubectl describe pod, kubectl logs). 2) Deployments — how Kubernetes keeps your Pods alive, scales them, and replaces them when they die (kubectl get deployments, kubectl rollout status). 3) Services — how traffic actually finds your Pods even as they get replaced (kubectl get svc, understand ClusterIP vs NodePort vs LoadBalancer). Only after those three feel boring and obvious, move to: 4) ConfigMaps and Secrets (configuration, not code, baked into images). 5) Ingress (routing traffic from outside the cluster). 6) Namespaces and RBAC (organizing and securing a real cluster). 7) StatefulSets, DaemonSets, Jobs/CronJobs (specialized workloads — you'll barely touch these your first few months). 8) Helm and Kustomize (templating, once you've written enough raw YAML to hate copy-pasting it). Don't skip ahead because a tutorial jumps there — skipping is exactly what burns people out in week one.
