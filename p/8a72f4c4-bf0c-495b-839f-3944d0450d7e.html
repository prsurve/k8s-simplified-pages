KCD

KCD stands for Kubernetes Community Days — regional, CNCF-backed events that bring the same KubeCon-style talks, workshops, and networking to your city, usually for a ticket price under $20-30 (sometimes free with sponsorship). The H1 2027 schedule is rolling out now city by city on kcd.community — bookmark that site and filter by your region or country. Each KCD page lists dates, CFP (call for papers) deadlines if you want to speak, and ticket links. If you're new: don't wait until you 'know Kubernetes well enough' to attend — go as a beginner, sit in on 2-3 talks that interest you, then spend the rest of the day in the hallway track talking to volunteers, speakers, and sponsors' booths. Most entry-level DevOps leads come from those 10-minute hallway conversations, not the talks themselves. If there's no KCD in your city yet, check the 'organize one' page — even volunteering to help run one looks great on a resume and gets you direct access to organizers who are usually senior engineers. Follow the page for your nearest city, set a calendar reminder for when tickets open, and go in with 3 goals: ask one speaker a question after their talk, exchange LinkedIn with at least 3 people, and bring a few business cards or a simple one-line pitch about what you're learning and looking for.
